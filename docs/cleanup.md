    To cleanup resources, go to [https://github.com/codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces){target=_blank} and delete the codespace.

=== "Run Locally"

    1. In the original VSCode window, go to the "Remote Explorer" panel (down the left hand side)
    
    2. Expand the "Dev Containers" category, hover over the obslab-k6 container and click the X to delete

You may also want to deactivate or delete the API token.
